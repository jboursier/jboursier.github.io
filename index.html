Email: boursier@mit.edu

Greetings! I am an Assistant Professor in the Mathematics Department at MIT. I graduated from Université Paris-Dauphine in 2022, where I did my PhD with Djalil Chafaï and Sylvia Serfaty.

My research is in probability theory, with a focus on statistical physics. I am currently interested in the Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless phase transition and in spin glasses.
